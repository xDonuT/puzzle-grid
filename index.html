How to Play

🎯 The Goal
Climb all 20 floors. Match tiles on your board to damage the rival before it out-damages you. Bosses await on floors 5, 10, 15, 20.
🎴 Tiles
⚔️ Sword — damage the rival · ⭐ Star — damage · ❤️ Heart — heal you · 🛡️ Shield — shield you · 🎲 Mystery — random buff or debuff (always a buff during Star Impact).
🧩 Shapes
3 in a row clears normally · 4 in a line = charged, double power · 5+ = star, extra strong · T/+ = cross seal — clears the full row + column and refunds 1 AP · L = X-seal — clears both diagonals and refunds 1 AP. Long runs also drop a Bloom tile that clears a 3×3 area.
⚡ AP & Turns
You get 3 actions (AP) per turn — each swap costs 1. Press End to pass play to the rival. Unused AP carries +1 into your next turn.
⭐ Your Signature Tile & Ultimate
Each hero has a signature tile: Ninja ⚔️, Wizard 🛡️, Knight ❤️. Matching it charges your ultimate — tap your portrait when it's ready. Matching more in one move charges more.
🌌 Phases
Star Fever (turn 6+): signature effects are doubled — the board turns yellow. Star Impact (turn 11+): mystery tiles always give a buff — the board turns orange.
🎭 Heroes
Ninja — dodges, lifesteals, devastating single-hit ultimate. Wizard — reflects damage, locks enemy shields, ultimate hits hard. Knight — regenerates, fractures enemies over time, tanky. Tap a portrait in battle for full details.
🎁 Rewards & Upgrades
Every floor clear grants a temporary perk for the next floor. Beating a boss lets you pick a permanent upgrade — some change how you play entirely. Elite floors (4, 9, 14, 19) give a permanent boost plus a rare perk.
Tip: you can check any status chip or read descriptions any time mid-battle.
