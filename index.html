How to Play

🎯 The Goal
Climb all 45 floors of the tower across three acts — 🌱 The Sprout (1–15), 🌸 The Bloom (16–30) and 🌺 The Flourish (31–45). Match tiles on your board to damage the rival before it out-damages you. Bosses wait on floors 15, 30 and 45.
🎴 Tiles
⚔️ Sword — damage · ⭐ Star — damage · ❤️ Heart — heal you · 🛡️ Shield — shield you · 🎲 Mystery — random buff or debuff (always a blessing during Full Bloom).
🧩 Shapes
3 in a row clears normally · 4 in a line = charged, double power · 5+ = star, extra strong · T/+ = cross seal — clears the full row + column and refunds 1 AP · L = X-seal — clears both diagonals and refunds 1 AP. Long runs also drop a Bloom tile that clears a 3×3 area.
⚡ AP & Turns
You get 3 actions (AP) per turn — each swap costs 1. Press Pass Turn to hand play to the rival. Unused AP carries +1 into your next turn.
🔀 Shuffling
Stuck? Shuffle re-deals the board — free every 3rd turn, otherwise 1 AP. If you own the 🌀 Shuffle Surge skill (Settings ▸ Global Skills), every shuffle empowers your next turn with stacking damage.
⭐ Signature Tile & Ultimate
Each hero has a signature tile: Ninja ⚔️, Wizard 🛡️, Knight ❤️. Matching it charges your ultimate — when the pips fill, tap your portrait to unleash it. Matching more in one move charges more.
🌌 Phases
Sun Surge (turn 6+): the sun pours down — signature effects are doubled. Full Bloom (turn 11+): the tower blooms — mystery tiles always give a blessing.
💚 Statuses
Chips under each portrait show what's afflicting them — ☠️ poison ticks each turn, 🦴 fracture bites at turn start, plus blinds, weakens and blessings. Tap any chip to read exactly what it does.
Tip: open the Action Log any time to relive the fight turn by turn.
🗺️ Between Battles
Each act is a small map — pick your path node by node. Mystery nodes flip risk-or-reward cards, shrines grant boons, and elites (floors 12, 27, 42) guard permanent boosts for those brave enough to fight them.
🎁 Rewards & Upgrades
Every floor clear grants a temporary perk for the next floor — plus a modifier you choose (benefit or challenge). Beating a boss (15, 30, 45) lets you pick a permanent upgrade and a passive from your hero's tree.
🌟 Golden Cosmos
Clear floor 45 once and the tower blooms gold forever. New Game+ offers Golden Loops: stronger rivals, richer recap stats, and an endless climb for glory. How far can the gold go?
🥷 Ninja
Blade-and-shadow duelist — dodges via Shadow Step, lifesteals on sword chains, and a devastating single-hit ultimate. Passive paths: Shadow, Venom, Blade, Afterglow.
🧙 Wizard
Star-weaver — reflects damage back, locks enemy shields, and channels an arcane ultimate. Passive paths: Runic, Mana, Arcana, Aegis.
🛡️ Knight
The unbroken wall — regenerates, fractures rivals over time, punishes every hit with counters. Passive paths: Fracture, Fortitude, Retaliate, Valor.
Tip: hold any portrait mid-battle to open its full passport — stats, costumes and quirks.
